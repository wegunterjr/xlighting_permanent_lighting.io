FAQ

Before you commit a roof.

Will this run Govee Pro lights?

Yes. Native 36V so you keep the kit's supply and pucks. WLED is pre-set for WS281x, SK6812, and Govee Pro RGBW / RGBWC. Confirm your connector and color order — Govee has shipped more than one revision.

Is WLED already on the board?

Yes. It leaves Utah pre-flashed. Join Wi-Fi, open the browser UI, and you have 100+ effects plus Home Assistant. Open-source (EUPL v1.2) — re-flash anytime.

What do the four channels do?

Four independent data outputs — A, B, C, and D on the board. Eaves, garage, porch, patio: different effects, different schedules, one enclosure.

How does the relay save power?

When the lights are off, a relay physically cuts power to the strings. They draw zero watts — not a fake-black that still sips 36V all summer. That's the difference on the electric bill.

How do I buy one?

First production run. Reserve with your email — no charge. We'll send a private $99.99 checkout link when your unit is ready to ship from Utah.

What's the warranty?

One year on craftsmanship. Support is local: info@utahlightcontrollers.com.
